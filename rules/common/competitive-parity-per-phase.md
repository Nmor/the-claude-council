# Parity checks

> Size budget: 2 KB.

For migrations and explicit parity work, maintain a capability/evidence ledger in the existing plan. Compare the changed capability and affected integration paths; do not repeat a full competitive survey at every phase. Reopen an accepted comparison only when scope, upstream contracts or evidence changes.

For relevant detailed procedures and examples, read
[the reference](../../rules-library/council-detail/competitive-parity-per-phase.md).
Do not preload it for unrelated work.
