# Specialist selection

> Size budget: 2 KB.

Select a specialist for an actual unresolved risk or independent deliverable. A keyword or touched filename alone does not justify a separate agent, a new research phase or all sixteen divisions. Security/auth, migrations, payments, public UX and operational changes still need their relevant checks. Report material findings and their evidence; omit ceremonial no-finding reports.

For relevant detailed procedures and examples, read
[the reference](../../rules-library/council-detail/council-triggers.md).
Do not preload it for unrelated work.
