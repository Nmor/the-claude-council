# Progress and handoff

> Size budget: 2 KB.

Keep a compact current-status section in the existing plan: objective, active worktree/commit, completed evidence, pending checks/blockers and next executable task. Update at meaningful milestones and handoff. Avoid duplicating the same narrative in plans, memory and session summaries. Surface material progress, not an account of every tool call.

For relevant detailed procedures and examples, read
[the reference](../../rules-library/council-detail/plan-execution-progress.md).
Do not preload it for unrelated work.
