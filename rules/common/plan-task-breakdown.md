# Useful planning

> Size budget: 2 KB.

For multi-step work, maintain small reviewable tasks in the existing authoritative plan. Preserve dependencies, acceptance criteria and integration work. A routine fix does not require a new plan, mandatory ADR or exhaustive questionnaire. Do not expand tasks only to give every division a role.

For relevant detailed procedures and examples, read
[the reference](../../rules-library/council-detail/plan-task-breakdown.md).
Do not preload it for unrelated work.
