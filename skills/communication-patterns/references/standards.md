## Standards Cited

### Structure + clarity

- **Barbara Minto — *The Pyramid Principle* (Financial Times /
  Prentice Hall, 3rd ed 2009, ISBN 978-0-273-71051-4; original
  1987)** — The answer-first / governing-thought / mutually-
  exclusive-collectively-exhaustive (MECE) structure used at
  McKinsey + Bain + BCG. The canonical framework for executive
  communication.
- **William Strunk Jr. + E.B. White — *The Elements of Style*
  (Pearson, 4th ed 2000, ISBN 978-0-205-30902-3; original 1959)**
  — Twelve rules of usage and eleven principles of composition
  that govern English prose. "Omit needless words" is rule 13.
- **Steven Pinker — *The Sense of Style* (Viking, 2014, ISBN
  978-0-670-02585-5)** — Modern psycholinguistics-informed style
  guide; the curse of knowledge problem; classic style.
- **Joseph M. Williams + Joseph Bizup — *Style: Lessons in
  Clarity and Grace* (Pearson, 12th ed 2017, ISBN
  978-0-13-408041-7)** — Sentence-level clarity craft;
  characters as subjects, actions as verbs.

### Narrative + persuasion

- **Chip Heath + Dan Heath — *Made to Stick* (Random House,
  2007, ISBN 978-1-4000-6428-1)** — SUCCESs framework for sticky
  ideas (Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional,
  Stories).
- **Chip Heath + Dan Heath — *Switch* (Broadway Books, 2010,
  ISBN 978-0-385-52875-7)** — Change communication; Rider /
  Elephant / Path framework.
- **Nancy Duarte — *Resonate* (Wiley, 2010, ISBN
  978-0-470-63201-7)** — Sparkline narrative structure (what is
  vs what could be, oscillating to "new bliss").
- **Nancy Duarte — *Slide:ology* (O'Reilly, 2008, ISBN
  978-0-596-52234-6)** — Visual storytelling for presentations.
- **Garr Reynolds — *Presentation Zen* (New Riders, 3rd ed
  2019, ISBN 978-0-13-580001-8; original 2008)** — Restraint +
  simplicity + Japanese aesthetic principles applied to slides.
- **Edward R. Tufte — *The Visual Display of Quantitative
  Information* (Graphics Press, 2nd ed 2001, ISBN
  978-0-9613921-4-7; original 1983)** + ***Envisioning
  Information* (Graphics Press, 1990) + *Beautiful Evidence*
  (Graphics Press, 2006)** — Data-ink ratio, chartjunk, sparklines,
  small multiples. The canonical reference for data visualisation.
- **Aristotle — *Rhetoric* (c. 350 BCE)** — Ethos / pathos /
  logos as the three appeals of persuasion. Still load-bearing
  2,400 years later.

### Conversation + interpersonal

- **Kerry Patterson + Joseph Grenny + Ron McMillan + Al
  Switzler — *Crucial Conversations* (McGraw-Hill, 3rd ed
  2022, ISBN 978-1-260-47418-3; original 2002)** — High-stakes
  conversations: start with heart, learn to look, make it safe,
  master your story, STATE the path, explore others' paths,
  move to action.
- **Douglas Stone + Bruce Patton + Sheila Heen — *Difficult
  Conversations* (Penguin, updated 2010, ISBN
  978-0-14-311844-2; original 1999)** — Harvard Negotiation
  Project follow-on; three conversations (What Happened, Feelings,
  Identity); learning conversation framework.
- **Dale Carnegie — *How to Win Friends and Influence People*
  (Pocket Books, 1981, ISBN 978-0-671-72365-2; original 1936)**
  — The foundational soft-skills text. Genuinely show interest,
  remember names, listen, find common ground. Still works
  because it's about substance, not manipulation.
- **Marshall B. Rosenberg — *Nonviolent Communication*
  (PuddleDancer, 3rd ed 2015, ISBN 978-1-892005-28-1)** —
  Observations / feelings / needs / requests — NVC framework for
  high-charge conversations.

### Listening

- **Kate Murphy — *You're Not Listening* (Celadon, 2020, ISBN
  978-1-250-29765-6)** — Listening as practice; why most people
  are bad at it; how to improve.
- **Michael P. Nichols + Martha B. Straus — *The Lost Art of
  Listening* (Guilford, 3rd ed 2021, ISBN
  978-1-4625-4264-6)** — Listening + relationship.

### Cross-cultural delivery

- **Erin Meyer — *The Culture Map* (PublicAffairs, 2014, ISBN
  978-1-61039-250-1)** — Eight scales of cultural difference
  including communicating (low-context vs high-context),
  evaluating (direct vs indirect feedback), persuading
  (principles-first vs applications-first), leading
  (egalitarian vs hierarchical), trusting (task-based vs
  relationship-based), disagreeing, scheduling, deciding.

### Bezos 6-pager + writing in operations

- **Jeff Bezos — Amazon Shareholder Letter 2017** — Codifies
  the Amazon "no PowerPoint, no presentations; write a six-
  page narrative memo" practice. Forces clear thinking. The
  meeting begins with silent reading of the document.
- **Will Larson — *An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering
  Management* (Stripe Press, 2019, ISBN 978-1-7322651-7-1)** —
  Practical engineering-leadership communication patterns.

### Crisis communication

- **Timothy Coombs — *Ongoing Crisis Communication* (SAGE, 6th
  ed 2023, ISBN 978-1-0719-0117-5)** — Situational Crisis
  Communication Theory (SCCT); attribution-of-responsibility
  framework; crisis response strategies.
- **Steven Fink — *Crisis Communications: The Definitive Guide*
  (McGraw-Hill, 2013, ISBN 978-0-07-179921-9)** — Crisis comms
  playbook.
