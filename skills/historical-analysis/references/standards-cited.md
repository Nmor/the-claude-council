## Standards Cited

- **Marc Bloch, *The Historian's Craft*** (Apologie pour l'histoire ou Métier d'historien,
  posthumous 1949; English translation Manchester UP 1954; reissued Vintage 1992; ISBN
  978-0394705125) — foundational text of the Annales school; sections on the nature of historical
  observation, source criticism, and historical causality
- **Fernand Braudel, *On History*** (Écrits sur l'histoire, 1969; English U Chicago Press 1980; ISBN
  978-0226071510) — longue durée, conjoncture, événementielle three-tier temporal framework
- **Jacques Le Goff, *History and Memory*** (Histoire et mémoire, 1977; English Columbia UP 1992;
  ISBN 978-0231075916) — memory as historical category; mentalités methodology
- **E. H. Carr, *What Is History?*** (Macmillan 1961; 40th anniversary ed Penguin 2018, ed. R. W.
  Davies; ISBN 978-0141993751) — the historian and the facts, society and the individual, history as
  progress chapters
- **John Tosh, *The Pursuit of History*** (7th ed Routledge 2024; 6th ed 2015 ISBN 978-1138808089) —
  standard methodology textbook; source criticism, historiographical traditions, modes of historical
  writing
- **Arthur Marwick, *The New Nature of History: Knowledge, Evidence, Language*** (Palgrave Macmillan
  2001; ISBN 978-0333964477) — defence of evidence-based history against postmodernist critique
- **Donald A. Ritchie, *Doing Oral History*** (3rd ed Oxford UP 2014; ISBN 978-0199329335) —
  interview design, transcription, ethics, archiving for oral history projects
- **Paul Thompson, *The Voice of the Past: Oral History*** (4th ed Oxford UP 2017, with Joanna
  Bornat; ISBN 978-0199335466) — theory and practice of oral history; reliability + complementarity
  with documentary sources
- **Alessandro Portelli, *The Death of Luigi Trastulli and Other Stories: Form and Meaning in Oral
  History*** (SUNY Press 1991; ISBN 978-0791404300) — interpretive framework for oral history;
  "errors" as historical evidence of memory and meaning
- **R. G. Collingwood, *The Idea of History*** (Oxford UP 1946; revised ed 1993, ed. Jan van der
  Dussen; ISBN 978-0192853066) — re-enactment thesis; history as the science of human action
- **Carlo Ginzburg, *The Cheese and the Worms*** (Il formaggio e i vermi, 1976; English Johns
  Hopkins UP 1980; ISBN 978-0801843877) — microhistory exemplar; archival reconstruction of a
  sixteenth-century miller's mentality
- **Eric Hobsbawm, *On History*** (Weidenfeld & Nicolson 1997; New Press 1997; ISBN 978-1565844858)
  — essays on Marxist historiography, longue durée, history from below
- **Lawrence Stone, *The Past and the Present Revisited*** (Routledge 1987; ISBN 978-0710209641) —
  revival of narrative; quantitative history's limits
- **Carmen M. Reinhart and Kenneth S. Rogoff, *This Time Is Different: Eight Centuries of Financial
  Folly*** (Princeton UP 2009; ISBN 978-0691142166) — long-run quantitative economic history
  (cliometrics) with a published-data spreadsheet error (Herndon-Ash-Pollin 2013) that became a
  methodological cautionary tale
- **William H. Sewell Jr., *Logics of History: Social Theory and Social Transformation*** (U Chicago
  Press 2005; ISBN 978-0226749181) — eventful temporality; structure and contingency
- **Niall Ferguson (ed.), *Virtual History: Alternatives and Counterfactuals*** (Picador 1997; ISBN
  978-0330351324) — disciplined counterfactual analysis
- **Chicago Manual of Style 17th edition** (U Chicago Press 2017; ISBN 978-0226287058) — humanities
  citation standard; notes-bibliography style used in history
- **ISAD(G) — General International Standard Archival Description** (2nd ed International Council on
  Archives 2000) — describes the multi-level archival description hierarchy (fonds, series, file,
  item)
- **DACS — Describing Archives: A Content Standard** (3rd ed Society of American Archivists 2022) —
  US implementation of archival description
- **EAD — Encoded Archival Description** (Library of Congress + Society of American Archivists,
  current schema EAD3 released 2015) — XML encoding for archival finding aids
- **Society of American Archivists Code of Ethics** (revised 2020) — provenance, access, privacy,
  intellectual property
- **Oral History Association Principles and Best Practices** (revised 2018, oralhistory.org) —
  informed consent, attribution, copyright, archiving
- **UNESCO Memory of the World Programme** (1992-) — international standards for documentary
  heritage preservation
- **L. P. Hartley, *The Go-Between*** (Hamish Hamilton 1953) — the line "The past is a foreign
  country: they do things differently there" used by David Lowenthal as the title of his 1985 work
  on historical consciousness

- **ISO 690:2021** — Information and documentation — guidelines
  for bibliographic references and citations
- **APA Publication Manual 7th Edition** — citation + research
  reporting (psychology / behavioural sciences default)
- **Chicago Manual of Style 17th Edition** — authoritative
  reference style (humanities + history)
- **IMRaD structure (ISO 215:1986)** — Introduction / Methods /
  Results / Discussion academic paper structure
- **PRISMA 2020 Statement** — Systematic reviews + meta-analyses
  reporting checklist
- **Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions** —
  Evidence synthesis methodology + risk-of-bias assessment
- **GRADE working group framework** — Evidence quality grading
  (High / Moderate / Low / Very Low)
- **OECD Frascati Manual 2015** — Standard practice for research
  - experimental development
- **AHA + APA + MLA citation styles** — Discipline-specific
  citation formats
- **Pyramid Principle (Barbara Minto)** — Top-down structured
  communication
- **Diátaxis Framework (Procida)** — Documentation organisation
  (tutorial / how-to / reference / explanation)
- **Nielsen Norman Group — User Research Methods** — Field studies,
  contextual inquiry, diary studies, usability testing, A/B
- **ISO 9241-210:2019** — Human-centred design for interactive
  systems
- **W3C WAI Research Methods** — Inclusive research practices
- **CWE-1426** — Improper validation of generative AI output
  (applies when AI assists synthesis)

### Cross-cutting research + documentation standards

- **ISO/IEC/IEEE 26515:2018** — Developing user documentation
  in an agile environment
- **ISO/IEC 25012:2008** — Data quality model (research data
  validation + traceability)
- **ISO/IEC 27037:2012** — Digital evidence handling (research
  artifact provenance + chain of custody)
- **NIST SP 800-92** — Log + audit-trail management (research
  methodology audit)
- **NIST SP 800-160 Vol 1** — Systems security engineering
  (when research artefacts inform system design)
- **OWASP ASVS 4.0.3 §V7.1** — Logging content (research +
  observation logs)
- **W3C ACT (Accessibility Conformance Testing)** — when
  research involves accessibility evaluation
- **CWE-1059** — Insufficient technical documentation
- **CWE-1295** — Debug messages revealing unnecessary information
  (applies to research dump-files + intermediate artefacts)
