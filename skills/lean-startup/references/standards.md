## Standards Cited

### Foundational

- **Eric Ries — *The Lean Startup* (Currency, 2011, ISBN
  978-0-307-88789-4)** — Defines validated learning, the build–
  measure–learn loop, innovation accounting (Chs 7-8), pivot/
  persevere (Ch 8), three engines of growth (Ch 10), small
  batches (Ch 9), and the "Startup = institution under
  extreme uncertainty" definition (Ch 2).
- **Eric Ries — *The Startup Way* (Currency, 2017, ISBN
  978-1-101-90320-9)** — Applying Lean Startup principles
  inside established enterprises ("intrapreneurship");
  introduces the **growth board** governance pattern and
  **metered funding** of internal ventures.
- **Steve Blank — *The Four Steps to the Epiphany* (K&S Ranch,
  2005/2013, ISBN 978-0-9764707-0-9)** — Original Customer
  Development methodology (Customer Discovery, Customer
  Validation, Customer Creation, Company Building). Ries
  explicitly credits Blank as Lean Startup's intellectual
  parent.
- **Steve Blank + Bob Dorf — *The Startup Owner's Manual*
  (K&S Ranch, 2012, ISBN 978-0-9849993-0-9)** — 608-page
  operational playbook for Customer Development with
  detailed checklists per phase.

### Business modelling

- **Alexander Osterwalder + Yves Pigneur — *Business Model
  Generation* (Wiley, 2010, ISBN 978-0-470-87641-1)** —
  Defines the Business Model Canvas (BMC) — 9 building blocks
  (Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Customer
  Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Resources, Key
  Activities, Key Partnerships, Cost Structure).
- **Alexander Osterwalder, Yves Pigneur, Greg Bernarda, Alan
  Smith — *Value Proposition Design* (Wiley, 2014, ISBN
  978-1-118-96807-1)** — Value Proposition Canvas (Customer
  Profile: jobs/pains/gains; Value Map: products+services /
  pain relievers / gain creators).
- **Ash Maurya — *Running Lean* (O'Reilly, 1st ed 2010, 2nd
  ed 2012, 3rd ed 2022, ISBN 978-1-09-810504-3)** — Lean
  Canvas (Maurya's modification of BMC: replaces Key
  Partners/Activities/Resources/Customer Relationships with
  Problem, Solution, Key Metrics, Unfair Advantage); Running
  Lean process loop.
- **Ash Maurya — *Scaling Lean* (Portfolio, 2016, ISBN
  978-1-101-98050-7)** — Customer Factory Blueprint; traction
  metrics; scaling once PMF is achieved.

### Product + discovery

- **Marty Cagan — *Inspired* (Wiley, 2nd ed 2017, ISBN
  978-1-119-38750-3)** — Product Management at world-class
  software companies; defines **product discovery vs
  product delivery** (dual-track agile); risk types
  (value, usability, feasibility, viability).
- **Marty Cagan + Chris Jones — *Empowered* (Wiley, 2020,
  ISBN 978-1-119-69129-7)** — Empowered product teams; OKRs
  for outcomes not outputs; team topologies.
- **Teresa Torres — *Continuous Discovery Habits* (Product
  Talk, 2021, ISBN 978-1-7345251-1-3)** — Weekly customer
  interviews; opportunity solution trees; assumption tests;
  outcome → opportunity → solution → assumption mapping.
- **Melissa Perri — *Escaping the Build Trap* (O'Reilly,
  2018, ISBN 978-1-491-97379-7)** — Defines **Build Trap**:
  measuring success by features shipped instead of customer
  outcomes; product kata; product strategy hierarchy.

### Growth

- **Sean Ellis + Morgan Brown — *Hacking Growth* (Currency,
  2017, ISBN 978-0-451-49721-4)** — Growth hacking process
  (PMF first via 40% rule survey, then build growth team,
  high-tempo testing, growth funnel optimisation —
  AARRR/Pirate Metrics: Acquisition, Activation, Retention,
  Revenue, Referral).
- **Dave McClure — Pirate Metrics (500 Startups, 2007)** —
  Original AARRR framework.

### Statistical / experimental

- **Diane Tang, Ashish Agarwal, Deirdre O'Brien, Mike
  Meyer — "Overlapping experiment infrastructure: More,
  better, faster experimentation"** (Google, KDD 2010)
  — A/B testing at scale, layered experiments.
- **Ronny Kohavi, Diane Tang, Ya Xu — *Trustworthy Online
  Controlled Experiments* (Cambridge, 2020, ISBN
  978-1-108-72426-5)** — Microsoft's experimentation manual;
  why most A/B tests are subtly broken; OEC (Overall
  Evaluation Criterion).

### Adjacent foundations

- **Clayton Christensen — *The Innovator's Dilemma* (HBS
  Press, 1997)** + ***Competing Against Luck* (Harper, 2016)
  — Jobs to be Done (JTBD); disruptive innovation theory.
- **Geoffrey Moore — *Crossing the Chasm* (HarperBusiness,
  3rd ed 2014, ISBN 978-0-06-229298-8)** — Technology
  Adoption Lifecycle (innovators, early adopters, early
  majority, late majority, laggards); the chasm between
  early adopters and early majority; bowling-alley + tornado
  patterns.
