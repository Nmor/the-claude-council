# Lean Startup — Verification Checklist

> The checks to run before claiming Lean Startup is being applied correctly,
> across hypothesis + MVP, innovation accounting, customer development,
> decision cadence, engine of growth, Build Trap avoidance, scaling
> discipline, and applicability. Pointed at by the **Verification Checklist**
> row of the SKILL.md reference map.
>
> **Size budget: 8 KB** — `token-budget.mjs --check`.

## Verification Checklist

Before claiming a Lean Startup methodology is being applied
correctly, verify ALL the following:

### Hypothesis & MVP

- [ ] Each major work stream has a written hypothesis (not
      "we believe people want X" but "we believe segment Y
      will pay $Z for solution W if it solves pain P")
- [ ] The riskiest assumption is identified explicitly
- [ ] The MVP for testing that assumption is the SMALLEST
      possible artifact — and we've considered no-code, video,
      concierge, and Wizard of Oz options before writing
      software
- [ ] The MVP defines the success/failure criterion BEFORE the
      experiment runs
- [ ] The MVP timeline is measured in days or weeks, not
      months

### Innovation accounting

- [ ] Vanity metrics are explicitly named and banned in
      reporting
- [ ] Cohort retention curves exist and are reviewed monthly
- [ ] LTV / CAC is calculated per cohort, not aggregate
- [ ] Activation rate, time-to-value, and DAU/MAU are tracked
- [ ] The North Star Metric is defined and traces to revenue
      / retention

### Customer Development

- [ ] At least 5 customer conversations per week per product
      trio
- [ ] Interview discipline (Mom Test / Torres story-based)
      followed — past-behaviour focus, not hypotheticals
- [ ] Opportunity Solution Tree exists for current outcome
- [ ] Findings logged + reviewed cross-team weekly

### Decision cadence

- [ ] Pivot-or-persevere meeting on the calendar at a regular
      cadence
- [ ] Each loop produces ≥1 piece of validated learning
- [ ] Hypotheses falsified are documented + cross-references
      future hypotheses

### Engine of growth

- [ ] One PRIMARY engine of growth named (sticky / viral /
      paid)
- [ ] Engine-specific KPIs tracked + reported
- [ ] No conflicting metrics across the three engines
      ("we want viral coefficient + low CAC + high retention"
      is fine; "we'll optimise all three simultaneously"
      reveals the team doesn't know which engine they have)

### Build Trap avoidance

- [ ] Roadmap describes outcomes, not features
- [ ] OKRs measure customer / business outcomes
- [ ] Discovery findings determine what to build (not founder
      whim or competitor's roadmap)
- [ ] Continuous discovery is running in parallel with delivery
      (dual-track agile)

### Scaling discipline

- [ ] Sean Ellis 40% PMF test result documented (or equivalent
      retention curve flatten) BEFORE scaling marketing /
      sales hires
- [ ] CAC payback < 12 months before scaling paid acquisition
- [ ] Hiring restraint enforced: no growth team until PMF; no
      sales scaling until repeatable acquisition motion

### Lean Startup applicability check

- [ ] The problem domain is genuinely uncertain (customer,
      value, or willingness-to-pay unknown). If it's an
      execution problem in a known market, Lean Startup is
      NOT the right tool — and that's explicitly recognised.
