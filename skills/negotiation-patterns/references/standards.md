## Standards Cited

### Foundational

- **Roger Fisher + William Ury + Bruce Patton — *Getting to Yes*
  (Houghton Mifflin Harcourt, 3rd ed 2011, ISBN 978-0-14-311875-6;
  original 1981)** — The Harvard Negotiation Project's principled
  / interest-based framework. The four prescriptions: (1)
  Separate the people from the problem; (2) Focus on interests,
  not positions; (3) Invent options for mutual gain; (4) Insist
  on objective criteria.
- **William Ury — *Getting Past No* (Bantam, 1991/2007, ISBN
  978-0-553-37131-5)** — Five-step "breakthrough" strategy for
  hard negotiations with intransigent counterparties: Go to the
  balcony; Step to their side; Reframe; Build them a golden bridge;
  Use power to educate.
- **William Ury — *The Power of a Positive No* (Bantam, 2007,
  ISBN 978-0-553-80498-1)** — The "Yes! No. Yes?" structure for
  saying no without rupturing the relationship.

### Tactical / behavioural

- **Chris Voss + Tahl Raz — *Never Split the Difference*
  (HarperBusiness, 2016, ISBN 978-0-06-240780-7)** — Tactical
  empathy: mirrors, labels, "no"-oriented questions, calibrated
  questions ("How am I supposed to do that?"), the "that's right"
  moment vs "you're right", the Ackerman bargaining model.
- **Stuart Diamond — *Getting More* (Crown Business, 2010, ISBN
  978-0-307-71690-3)** — Diamond's 12 strategies and the "pictures
  in their head" principle.
- **Deepak Malhotra + Max Bazerman — *Negotiation Genius*
  (Bantam, 2007, ISBN 978-0-553-38411-8)** — Behavioural-economics-
  informed tactics, contingency contracts, multiple equivalent
  simultaneous offers (MESOs), post-settlement settlements.
- **Adam Galinsky + Maurice Schweitzer — *Friend & Foe* (Crown,
  2015, ISBN 978-0-307-72020-7)** — Cooperation + competition;
  when each dominates.

### Integrative / multi-issue

- **David Lax + James Sebenius — *3-D Negotiation* (HBS Press,
  2006, ISBN 978-1-59139-799-9)** — Three dimensions: (1)
  Tactics at the table; (2) Deal design; (3) Setup — choosing the
  right parties, sequence, and issues.
- **David Lax + James Sebenius — *The Manager as Negotiator*
  (Free Press, 1986, ISBN 978-0-02-918770-3)** — The
  value-claiming / value-creating tension; how aggressive
  claiming destroys integrative potential.
- **Howard Raiffa — *The Art and Science of Negotiation*
  (Belknap, 1982, ISBN 978-0-674-04812-3)** — The analytic
  foundation; reservation values; ZOPA; quantitative tradeoff
  analysis.
- **Howard Raiffa, John Richardson, David Metcalfe —
  *Negotiation Analysis* (Belknap, 2002, ISBN
  978-0-674-00890-5)** — Modern analytic update.

### Mediation + conflict resolution

- **Lawrence Susskind + Jeffrey Cruikshank — *Breaking Robert's
  Rules* (Oxford, 2006, ISBN 978-0-19-530841-7)** — Consensus
  building processes.
- **Lawrence Susskind + Patrick Field — *Dealing with an Angry
  Public* (Free Press, 1996, ISBN 978-0-684-82302-6)** — Public
  controversy + mutual-gains approach.
- **Robert Mnookin, Scott Peppet, Andrew Tulumello — *Beyond
  Winning* (Belknap, 2000, ISBN 978-0-674-00231-6)** —
  Negotiation with lawyers; agency problems.

### Cross-cultural

- **Geert Hofstede + Gert Jan Hofstede + Michael Minkov —
  *Cultures and Organizations* (McGraw-Hill, 3rd ed 2010, ISBN
  978-0-07-166418-9)** — Six cultural dimensions (Power
  Distance, Individualism vs Collectivism, Masculinity vs
  Femininity, Uncertainty Avoidance, Long-term Orientation,
  Indulgence vs Restraint) and their effect on bargaining
  style.
- **Erin Meyer — *The Culture Map* (PublicAffairs, 2014, ISBN
  978-1-61039-250-1)** — Eight scales of cultural difference
  including disagreeing (confrontational vs avoids
  confrontation) and persuading (principles-first vs
  applications-first).
- **Jeanne Brett — *Negotiating Globally* (Jossey-Bass, 3rd ed
  2014, ISBN 978-1-118-60260-6)** — Joint gains across cultures.

### Salary + offer-specific

- **Linda Babcock + Sara Laschever — *Women Don't Ask*
  (Bantam, 2007, ISBN 978-0-553-38387-6)** — Gender + initiation
  of negotiation; effect on compensation outcomes.
- **Patrick McKenzie ("patio11") — "Salary Negotiation: Make
  More Money, Be More Valued" (kalzumeus.com, 2012)** — The
  canonical engineer-salary-negotiation essay.

### Ethics

- **Roy Lewicki, David Saunders, Bruce Barry — *Negotiation*
  (McGraw-Hill, 8th ed 2020, ISBN 978-1-260-04344-3)** — The
  textbook; tactics catalogue + ethics taxonomy.
- **G. Richard Shell — *Bargaining for Advantage*
  (Penguin, 3rd ed 2018, ISBN 978-0-14-313056-7)** — Style-aware
  negotiation; ethical bright lines (the "lie about a material
  fact" test).
