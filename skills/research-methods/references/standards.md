## Standards Cited

### Foundational

- **Wayne C. Booth + Gregory G. Colomb + Joseph M. Williams +
  Joseph Bizup + William T. FitzGerald — *The Craft of
  Research* (University of Chicago Press, 4th ed 2016, ISBN
  978-0-226-23973-6; original 1995)** — The canonical
  textbook for designing research projects: question →
  problem → solution; sources; arguments; warrants.
- **John W. Creswell + J. David Creswell — *Research Design:
  Qualitative, Quantitative, and Mixed Methods Approaches*
  (SAGE, 6th ed 2023, ISBN 978-1-0718-1779-7; original
  1994)** — Comprehensive design textbook covering the three
  main paradigms.
- **Joseph A. Maxwell — *Qualitative Research Design: An
  Interactive Approach* (SAGE, 3rd ed 2013, ISBN
  978-1-4129-8119-4; original 1996)** — Interactive design;
  goals / conceptual framework / research questions / methods /
  validity as a single system.
- **Earl Babbie — *The Practice of Social Research* (Cengage,
  16th ed 2024, ISBN 978-0-357-36079-6; original 1973)** — The
  canonical undergraduate methods textbook.

### Specific methods

- **Robert K. Yin — *Case Study Research and Applications*
  (SAGE, 6th ed 2017, ISBN 978-1-5063-3617-0; original
  1984)** — Case-study methodology: single + multiple case,
  embedded vs holistic, construct + internal + external
  validity, reliability.
- **Barney G. Glaser + Anselm L. Strauss — *The Discovery of
  Grounded Theory* (Aldine, 1967, reprinted Routledge 2017,
  ISBN 978-0-202-30260-7)** — Grounded theory; constant
  comparison; theoretical sampling.
- **Virginia Braun + Victoria Clarke — "Using thematic
  analysis in psychology" (Qualitative Research in Psychology
  3(2):77-101, 2006)** — The canonical paper on thematic
  analysis; 6-phase method.
- **Anselm Strauss + Juliet Corbin — *Basics of Qualitative
  Research* (SAGE, 4th ed 2014, ISBN 978-1-4129-9747-8)** —
  Open / axial / selective coding for grounded theory.
- **Don A. Dillman + Jolene D. Smyth + Leah Melani Christian
  — *Internet, Phone, Mail, and Mixed-Mode Surveys: The
  Tailored Design Method* (Wiley, 4th ed 2014, ISBN
  978-1-118-45614-9)** — The canonical survey methodology
  text; questionnaire design, response error reduction, mode
  selection.

### Statistics + inference

- **Andrew Gelman + Jennifer Hill — *Data Analysis Using
  Regression and Multilevel/Hierarchical Models* (Cambridge,
  2007, ISBN 978-0-521-68689-1) + *Regression and Other
  Stories* (Gelman + Hill + Vehtari, 2020, ISBN
  978-1-107-67650-2)** — Modern Bayesian-flavoured statistics.
- **Judea Pearl — *Causality* (Cambridge, 2nd ed 2009, ISBN
  978-0-521-89560-6) + *The Book of Why* (Basic, 2018, ISBN
  978-0-465-09760-9)** — Causal inference; DAGs;
  do-calculus; "no causes in, no causes out".
- **Joshua D. Angrist + Jörn-Steffen Pischke — *Mostly
  Harmless Econometrics* (Princeton, 2008, ISBN
  978-0-691-12035-5) + *Mastering Metrics* (Princeton, 2014,
  ISBN 978-0-691-15284-4)** — Practical causal-inference
  toolkit: RCT, IV, regression discontinuity, diff-in-diff.
- **Ronald A. Fisher — *The Design of Experiments* (Oliver +
  Boyd, 1935)** + **George E. P. Box + J. Stuart Hunter +
  William G. Hunter — *Statistics for Experimenters* (Wiley,
  2nd ed 2005, ISBN 978-0-471-71813-0)** — Experimental
  design foundation.

### Systematic review + meta-analysis

- **Cochrane Collaboration — *Cochrane Handbook for Systematic
  Reviews of Interventions* (Higgins et al., 6.4, 2023)** —
  The canonical methodology for evidence synthesis.
- **PRISMA 2020 (Page et al., BMJ 2021;372:n71)** — Preferred
  Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses.
- **Mark Petticrew + Helen Roberts — *Systematic Reviews in
  the Social Sciences* (Wiley-Blackwell, 2006, ISBN
  978-1-4051-2110-1)** — SR methodology beyond healthcare.

### Bias + replication

- **John P. A. Ioannidis — "Why Most Published Research
  Findings Are False" (PLoS Medicine 2(8):e124, 2005)** — The
  paper that crystallised the replication crisis.
- **Open Science Collaboration — "Estimating the
  reproducibility of psychological science" (Science 349,
  2015)** — The Many Labs replication project.
- **Brian Wansink case (2018 onward)** — Cornell food research
  retractions; pattern of p-hacking + HARKing (Hypothesising
  After Results are Known).

### Ethics + integrity

- **Belmont Report (US National Commission, 1979)** — The
  foundational document for research ethics with human
  subjects: respect for persons, beneficence, justice.
- **Declaration of Helsinki (WMA, 1964, last amended 2013)** —
  Ethical principles for medical research with human
  subjects.
- **International Committee of Medical Journal Editors (ICMJE)
  — *Recommendations for the Conduct, Reporting, Editing, and
  Publication of Scholarly Work in Medical Journals* (2019)** —
  Authorship, conflict-of-interest, peer review, data sharing.

### Data + reproducibility

- **FAIR Principles — Findable, Accessible, Interoperable,
  Reusable (Wilkinson et al., Scientific Data 3:160018, 2016)
  - Research Data Alliance recommendations** — Standard for
  scientific data management.
- **Open Science Framework (cos.io)** — Pre-registration +
  data sharing platform.
- **OECD Frascati Manual 2015 (ISBN 978-92-64-23901-2)** —
  Definition + measurement of R&D activities.

- **ISO 690:2021** — Information and documentation — guidelines
  for bibliographic references and citations
- **APA Publication Manual 7th Edition** — citation + research
  reporting (psychology / behavioural sciences default)
- **Chicago Manual of Style 17th Edition** — authoritative
  reference style (humanities + history)
- **IMRaD structure (ISO 215:1986)** — Introduction / Methods /
  Results / Discussion academic paper structure
- **PRISMA 2020 Statement** — Systematic reviews + meta-analyses
  reporting checklist
- **Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions** —
  Evidence synthesis methodology + risk-of-bias assessment
- **GRADE working group framework** — Evidence quality grading
  (High / Moderate / Low / Very Low)
- **OECD Frascati Manual 2015** — Standard practice for research
  - experimental development
- **AHA + APA + MLA citation styles** — Discipline-specific
  citation formats
- **Pyramid Principle (Barbara Minto)** — Top-down structured
  communication
- **Diátaxis Framework (Procida)** — Documentation organisation
  (tutorial / how-to / reference / explanation)
- **Nielsen Norman Group — User Research Methods** — Field studies,
  contextual inquiry, diary studies, usability testing, A/B
- **ISO 9241-210:2019** — Human-centred design for interactive
  systems
- **W3C WAI Research Methods** — Inclusive research practices
- **CWE-1426** — Improper validation of generative AI output
  (applies when AI assists synthesis)

### Cross-cutting research + documentation standards

- **ISO/IEC/IEEE 26515:2018** — Developing user documentation
  in an agile environment
- **ISO/IEC 25012:2008** — Data quality model (research data
  validation + traceability)
- **ISO/IEC 27037:2012** — Digital evidence handling (research
  artifact provenance + chain of custody)
- **NIST SP 800-92** — Log + audit-trail management (research
  methodology audit)
- **NIST SP 800-160 Vol 1** — Systems security engineering
  (when research artefacts inform system design)
- **OWASP ASVS 4.0.3 §V7.1** — Logging content (research +
  observation logs)
- **W3C ACT (Accessibility Conformance Testing)** — when
  research involves accessibility evaluation
- **CWE-1059** — Insufficient technical documentation
- **CWE-1295** — Debug messages revealing unnecessary information
  (applies to research dump-files + intermediate artefacts)
